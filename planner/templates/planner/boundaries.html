Area / neighbourhood names (Cantt, Gulgasht, Model Town…)

Every site is labelled with the area it sits in, shown on the maps, in site pop-ups, the Islands list, the Consolidated table, Excel and KML. Built in: ~73,000 neighbourhoods, towns and villages across Punjab (Who's On First / GeoNames). City neighbourhoods are patchy there, so add your own: they always win. Three ways:

  1. On the planner map: click ＋ Area name above the maps, click the spot, type the name (e.g. Gulgasht).
  2. Excel / CSV with columns Name, Latitude, Longitude (and optional City) — one row per area.
  3. KML / KMZ from Google Earth or My Maps: drop a pin or draw a shape per area and name it. Sites inside a shape get that name; pins name sites within 2 km.
